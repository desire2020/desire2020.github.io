\cvsection{Research Interests}

\begin{cvparagraph}
My long-term research interest lies in \textbf{measure-theoretic questions about language}, especially how measures over language shape model intelligence, generalization, and training efficiency in reinforcement learning. My work on \textbf{non-autoregressive modeling} and \textbf{post-training} follows this same line of inquiry.

I develop practical models and algorithms grounded in theoretical insight, with applications to reinforcement learning, continual and test-time learning, controllable generation, and language-model architectures beyond autoregressive Transformers.
\end{cvparagraph}
