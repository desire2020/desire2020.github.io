## Authors

**Sidi Lu**, Jiayuan Mao, Joshua Tenenbaum, and Jiajun Wu

## Overview

Neurally-Guided Structure Inference, or NG-SI, combines the robustness of explicit search with the efficiency of learned inference. A neural network guides a hierarchical, layer-wise search over a compositional structure space instead of predicting the complete structure in one step.

This hybrid design is intended to generalize beyond the structural complexity seen during training without paying the full cost of exhaustive search.

## Results

The method is evaluated on probabilistic matrix decomposition and symbolic program parsing. On both tasks, it outperforms purely data-driven and purely search-based alternatives.
